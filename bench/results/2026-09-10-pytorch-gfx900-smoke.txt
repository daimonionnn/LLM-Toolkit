Notes
-----
Every check compares against a CPU reference or a known distribution rather
than testing for the absence of an exception: a kernel that silently returned
zeros would pass a "did it crash" test.

1.40 TFLOP/s is about 57% of this iGPU's theoretical fp32 peak
(8 CU x 64 lanes x 2 flop x 2.4 GHz = 2.46 TFLOP/s).

rocRAND passing is the notable one. Issue #1 reported it as blocked on gfx900
under modular ROCm 7.14.1, where its kernels ship inside a .kpack container.
The torch 2.7.0+rocm6.3 wheel bundles its own rocRAND with gfx900 code, so the
packaging question never arises.

No Tensile backport and no source build were involved. The requirements are
the stock wheel, HSA_OVERRIDE_GFX_VERSION=9.0.0, and passing the render/video
groups by numeric gid (see run/run-pytorch-rocm63.sh).
